Alternative: Operational Data Flow

TableTap 4-Actor Operational Data Flow

Clean, Zero-Gap Vertical Swimlanes (Customer → Kitchen → Waiter → Management)

1. DINER (Any Browser)
Step 1: Scan Table QR Opens digital menu in mobile browser without app install.
Step 2: Browse & Customize Dietary filters, spice levels, allergens, and ML dish recommendations.
Step 3: Cart & Checkout Calculates 5% GST, loyalty points redemption, and dispatch.
Step 4: Post-Dining Feedback Submits ratings; ABSA engine alerts kitchen on issues.
Outputs: Orders & Reviews
2. CHEF (Kitchen KDS)
Step 1: Ticket Arrival Chime Web Audio API plays dual-frequency chime upon WebSocket order insert.
Step 2: Order Partitioning Auto-splits items: Cooked Food tab vs. Ready-Made drinks/dessert tab.
Step 3: FIFO Queue Management Chronological sorting with >15 min overdue pulse alert.
Step 4: Mark "Ready to Serve" Dispatches pickup event directly to Waitstaff console.
Outputs: Preparation Status
3. WAITER (Floor Console)
Step 1: Shift Authentication Morning / Evening / Night shift login with assigned table sections.
Step 2: Table Service Dispatch Receives live guest requests (Water, Bill, Cutlery, Clean Table).
Step 3: Dish Pickup Alert Alerted when KDS marks food "Ready to Serve" with exact table number.
Step 4: Mark "Delivered" Completes ticket lifecycle and updates table turnover metrics.
Outputs: Service Resolution
4. ADMIN (Management)
Step 1: Live BI Dashboard Monitors daily gross sales, AOV, RevPASH, and active table turnover.
Step 2: Menu & Stock Editor Sets prices, manages inventory par levels, toggles daily availability.
Step 3: Bhakasura AI Copilot Asks operational queries; RAG answers via AST-sandboxed Supabase SQL.
Step 4: Automated Exports Generates PDF tax invoices and styled Excel management reports.
Outputs: Executive Decisions
